Membership

Become a Maunga Club member.

What to send

To apply for membership, send the club the following information:

  1. Full name
  2. Physical address
  3. Proof of address
  4. Proof of identification

Club email: maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub

Keep your information safe

Proof of address and identification contain personal information. These documents should only be sent directly to the club.

This website does not collect or upload personal documents.

Membership will be confirmed by the club after the application has been reviewed.
