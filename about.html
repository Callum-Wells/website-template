About Maunga Club

More time on the mountain.

Stay close to the slopes

Maunga Club provides shared ski accommodation for club members. There are three club chalets located on the mountain.

Members can stay close to the skiing and snowboarding area, making it easier to get to and from their accommodation.

The mountain has around 550 hectares of skiing and snowboarding area and has a long ski season.

Before you plan a stay

  • Members only. Only Maunga Club members can access the chalets.
  • Friday to Sunday. Chalet bookings are made from Friday to Sunday.
  • Check the capacity. Each chalet has a different maximum number of adults and children.
  • Shared accommodation. The three chalets can accommodate groups ranging from 6 to 40 people.
